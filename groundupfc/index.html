Ground Up FC

You are not the manager. You are the chairman.

Build your club from the ground up

Take over any club in a seven-league football pyramid, or start your own in non-league with your own name, colours and crest. Sign the players, hire the coach, build the stadium and keep the board and the fans on your side.

What you do

Transfers

Agree the fee with the club, then the wages and the contract. Sell when the price is right.

The coach

Hire the head coach, and fire him when it goes wrong.

The stadium

Build it stand by stand: roof, floodlights, kiosks, fan shop, VIP lounge and a big screen.

Money

Set ticket prices, sell season tickets and sign sponsors. Spend more than you earn and the bank says no.

The academy

Bring through your own teenagers and give the best of them a chance.

Match day

Watch the match live, jump to the result, or simulate to the end of the season.

Good to know

More than 160 clubs, cups, play-offs and two European competitions. Eight languages. Plays offline. Free to play with optional purchases and a short ad break twice a season.
